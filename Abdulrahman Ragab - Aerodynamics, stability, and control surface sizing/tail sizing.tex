\section{Introduction \& System Overview}
This document outlines the complete aerodynamic design, trade study, and sizing workflow for a custom RC aircraft with a 25-inch wingspan, a target total mass of $514\,\text{g}$, a Mean Aerodynamic Chord ($\text{MAC}$) of $0.13\,\text{m}$, and an Aspect Ratio $AR_{W} = 4.96$. The primary objective is to establish static longitudinal stability ($SM = 0.16$), a balanced center of gravity ($x_{CG} = 0.07\,\text{m}$ from the leading edge), and a volume tail coefficient $V_{HT} = 0.7$.

\section{Horizontal Tail Sizing \& Optimum Length Calculation}
To size the horizontal tail (HT) based on the optimized main wing data and maintain a tail volume coefficient of $V_{HT} = 0.7$, an analytical sizing procedure was implemented using fuselage dimensions and wing geometry ($\text{MAC} = 0.13\,\text{m}$, $S_w = 0.08001\,\text{m}^2$).

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Fuselage cross section Length ($L_f$):} $0.085\,\text{m}$
    \item \textbf{Equivalent Fuselage Diameter ($D_f$):} Evaluated via cross-sectional area $A_f$ as:
    \begin{equation}
        D_f = \sqrt{\frac{4 A_f}{\pi}} = 0.096\,\text{m}
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Optimum Tail Arm Length ($L_{opt}$):} Derived from stability requirements and body geometry:
    \begin{equation}
        L_{opt} = \sqrt{\frac{4 \cdot \text{MAC} \cdot S_w \cdot V_{HT}}{\pi \cdot D_f}} = 0.311\,\text{m}
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Required Horizontal Tail Area ($S_{HT}$):} Calculated using the tail volume relation:
    \begin{equation}
        S_{HT} = \frac{V_{HT} \cdot \text{MAC} \cdot S_w}{L_{opt}} = 0.0234\,\text{m}^2
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Aspect Ratio ($AR_{ht}$):} Scaled relative to the main wing aspect ratio ($AR_w$):
    \begin{equation}
        AR_{ht} = \frac{2}{3} AR_w = 3.306
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Horizontal Tail Wingspan ($b_{ht}$):} Derived from the aspect ratio and surface area:
    \begin{equation}
        b_{ht} = \sqrt{AR_{ht} \cdot S_{HT}} = 0.278\,\text{m}
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Root Chord ($C_{r,ht}$):}
    \begin{equation}
        S_{HT} = \frac{b_{ht}}{2} (1 + \lambda) C_{r,ht}
    \end{equation}
\end{itemize}


\section{Mass Distribution \& Center of Gravity (CG) Tuning}
To resolve simulation inertia errors and align the system CG with the target $x_{CG} = 0.0742\,\text{m}$, component weights and point masses were distributed as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Main Wing:} $125\,\text{g}$ (at $X = 0.073\,\text{m}$)[cite: 1]
    \item \textbf{Fuselage and Hardware:} $134\,\text{g}$ (at $X = 0.08\,\text{m}$)
    \item \textbf{Horizontal Tail (HT):} $18\,\text{g}$ (at $X = 0.376\,\text{m}$)
    \item \textbf{Vertical Tail (VT):} $10\,\text{g}$ (at $X = 0.348\,\text{m}$)
    \item \textbf{Battery:} $75\,\text{g}$ (at $X = 0.010\,\text{m}$)
    \item \textbf{Motor:} $28\,\text{g}$ (at $X = -0.050\,\text{m}$)
    \item \textbf{Avionics \& Electronics:} $124\,\text{g}$ (distributed around $X = 0.050\,\text{m}$ to $0.090\,\text{m}$)
    \item \textbf{Total System Mass:} $514\,\text{g}$
\end{itemize}

\section{Vertical Tail Aspect Ratio (AR) Trade Study}
To determine the optimal aspect ratio ($AR$) for the vertical tail, a weighted trade study was conducted evaluating five distinct aspect ratios ($AR = 1.3, 1.5, 1.7, 1.9,$ and $2.1$) with a fixed surface area and a tail arm length of $L_{opt} = 0.311\,\text{m}$ at $V = 20.0\,\text{m/s}$ using VLM2. 

\subsection{Vertical Tail Sizing \& Numerical Derivation}
Using the vertical tail volume coefficient relation alongside the main wing and fuselage geometry, the physical dimensions of the vertical tail are calculated explicitly:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Vertical Tail Area ($S_{VT}$):} 
    \begin{equation}
        S_{VT} = \frac{V_{VT} \cdot S_w \cdot b_w}{L_{opt}} = \frac{0.04 \times 0.08\,\text{m}^2 \times 0.63\,\text{m}}{0.311\,\text{m}} \approx 0.00648\,\text{m}^2
    \end{equation}
    
    \item \textbf{Vertical Tail Span ($b_{VT}$):} Derived using the selected optimal aspect ratio:
    \begin{equation}
        b_{VT} = \sqrt{AR \cdot S_{VT}}
    \end{equation}
    
    \item \textbf{Root Chord ($C_{r, VT}$):} Determined using the initial taper ratio ($\lambda = 0.6$):
    \begin{equation}
        S_{VT} = \frac{b_{VT}}{2} (1 + \lambda) C_{r, VT} \implies C_{r, VT} = \frac{2 \cdot S_{VT}}{b_{VT} (1 + \lambda)}
    \end{equation}
    
    \item \textbf{Tip Chord ($C_{t, VT}$):} 
    \begin{equation}
        C_{t, VT} = C_{r, VT} \cdot \lambda
    \end{equation}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Trade study}

Each configuration was evaluated and scored on a relative scale from $-2$ (worst performance / severe oscillations) to $+2$ (best performance / ideal linear response) across core stability criteria:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Directional Stability Slope ($C_{n\beta}$ linearity, Weight: 40\%):} Evaluates the magnitude and linearity of the yaw restoring moment gradient to ensure proper weathercock stability.
    \item \textbf{Rolling-Yaw Coupling ($C_{l\beta}$ smoothness, Weight: 30\%):} Measures unwanted side-slip rolling moment oscillations around zero slip.
    \item \textbf{Structural Rigidity \& Flutter Margin (Weight: 30\%):} Assesses aspect ratio impacts on structural stiffness and root bending stress.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Selection Conclusion}
Based on the XFLR5 graphical results, the **$AR = 1.5$** configuration achieves the highest total score ($+1.70$). It demonstrates a clean, smooth, linear positive slope in $C_{n\beta}$ without the severe instabilities and high-frequency oscillations observed at higher or lower aspect ratios. Consequently, $AR = 1.5$ is selected as the optimal vertical tail aspect ratio before proceeding to the taper ratio optimization.

\textbf{Selected Parameters:}

\begin{itemize}
    \item AR = 1.5
    \item $b_{VT} = 0.099 m$
    \item $S_{VT} = 6.48*10^{-3} m^2$
\end{itemize}
